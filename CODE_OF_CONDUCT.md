# Code of conduct

Be kind, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks aren't welcome anywhere in this project.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and block people who don't follow this. Report problems privately to the maintainer via [rishishah.in](https://rishishah.in).

This is a short form of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
